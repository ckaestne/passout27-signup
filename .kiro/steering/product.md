# Product

Registration site for the North American PassOut 2027, a residential
club-passing juggling convention (Park Hills, MO, April 10–18, 2027,
max. 40 attendees). Three volunteer organizers run it.

It is a minimal web shop without payment: people reserve a place and a
sleeping option, see the price live, and the organizers confirm or cancel.
Payment happens outside the app.

It replaces a Google Form. Keep it as simple as a Google Form for attendees
and organizers. All prices, quantities and texts come from a Google
Spreadsheet, never from code.
