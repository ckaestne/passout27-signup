PassOut is an event for passionate passers. Most activities will revolve
around all forms of club passing, from 4-handed siteswaps to takeout
patterns, with some ball and ring passing maybe mixed in -- solo juggling
is rare. The week will focus on learning, teaching, and inventing new
patterns, building community together by cooking and cleaning for the
group, playing board games and doing puzzles, playing volleyclub, and
possibly taking an outing together. There are usually many workshops
throughout the week.

PassOut is open to bringing family members and non-jugglers along with you.
We all pitch in to prepare meals together, so consider bringing a recipe
that scales well and includes vegetarian and vegan options.

Never been to PassOut before and wondering if you're "good enough" to
come? Ask yourself, would you enjoy doing nothing but passing for a whole
week, and do you love learning new patterns? If yes, then you should come.
More at [passout.club](https://passout.club).
