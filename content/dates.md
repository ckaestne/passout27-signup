PassOut is from April 10 to April 18. There is no public transit to the
site. If you're not driving, get yourself to St. Louis and we'll coordinate
rides on the first and last day.

We encourage people to attend for the entire week and most of our costs are
fixed. We reduce the registration fee for people arriving late or leaving
early for each missed night. For people only attending for a night or two,
we charge per night.
